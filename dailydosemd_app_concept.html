Daily curriculum, drip-released
One post per day unlocks, matching the Instagram arc. The streak system creates the same habit loop as Duolingo — but for clinical knowledge. System chips let users jump between organ systems at any time. Completed posts grey out; upcoming ones show a teaser.

Flashcard mode built from every post
Each Instagram post auto-generates a deck of active-recall cards — the vignette questions, the mnemonic, the cheat sheet table rows. Users swipe through with spaced repetition (again / hard / got it). The progress bar shows position in the current post's deck. Cards resurface at optimal intervals, like Anki — but the content is already written and curated.

Trial Friday as a database
Each Trial Friday post becomes a structured record in a searchable trial library. Users can filter by organ system, year, or Step relevance. The "Step trigger" row at the bottom is the exam shortcut — exactly what an examiner tests about this trial. Over time this becomes the most useful landmark trial reference in medical education.
feature overview — what makes it different from iSnhorts
Daily drip, not a dump
One post unlocks per day — no overwhelming library. The scarcity creates the habit.
Curriculum arc, not random facts
Content builds across the week. Monday's buzzword feeds Wednesday's vignette. Friday's trial explains why.
Auto-generated Anki decks
Every post becomes a spaced-repetition deck. No import, no setup — just study.
Dual-purpose by design
Every card tags both the Step level and the ward application. One app for board prep and clinical work.
Trial library
Every Trial Friday becomes a searchable record. The only landmark trial database designed for Step and the wards simultaneously.
Streak + social accountability
Daily streak, weekly completion rate, shareable progress cards. Study alone, but feel part of a cohort.
